Frequently asked questions

Do I have to stop eating the foods I love?+

No. The guide shows how to approach familiar foods and everyday meals. The goal is more balanced habits, not eliminating your culture or favourite foods.

Do I need to go to the gym?+

No. The guide explores walking, home workouts, dancing, stair climbing and strength exercises.

Will this work with a busy work schedule?+

Yes. It was created with working women and their everyday responsibilities in mind, and focuses on habits that fit around a busy schedule.

What exactly am I buying?+

The main ebook, the companion workbook, the listed planning resources and community access.

Is this a physical book?+

No. It’s a digital product you can open on a compatible phone, tablet or computer.

How quickly will I lose weight?+

Results vary from person to person. The guide doesn’t promise a specific amount of weight loss or a timeline. It gives you practical education and tools to build healthier habits.

Do I get access to a community?+

Yes. Community access is included. Joining details are provided after purchase.

Do I have to pay every month?+

No. It’s a one-time payment, with no monthly subscription.

What if I have a medical condition or special dietary needs?+

The guide is general education, not individual medical advice. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or have specific health needs, speak with a qualified healthcare professional before making significant changes to your eating or exercise.
